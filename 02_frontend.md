# Registration and Queue

## Registration

- Anyone intending to use a laundry machine registers on the queue site.
- Registration requires a name only; no account or other identity check is required.
- The system assigns each registrant a queue number from a daily registration counter.
- The counter resets at midnight EST.
- The queue page displays the names and queue numbers of people using machines or waiting.

## Choosing a machine or waiting

- A user may choose any machine shown as available.
- If all usable machines are occupied, users wait in first-come, first-served order.
- A machine marked out of order is not available for selection.

## Claiming an available machine

- When a machine becomes available and someone is waiting, the next person in line is notified and has 10 minutes to claim it.
- If that person does not claim the machine within 10 minutes, they move back one position in the queue, allowing the next person to proceed.

## Unspecified

- Whether a user can cancel registration or leave the queue, and how cancellation affects their queue number or position, has not been decided.
- The action a user takes to claim a machine, and what happens if a claim window expires when no other person is waiting, have not been specified.
